## What "pixels" as the unit of replication means for the p-values

Each pixel counted as one observation, with 792 pixels per region. Pixels that are near each other are correlated, so they are not independent replicates.

- The p-values and q-values are too small (anti-conservative). Both tools gave this pseudoreplication warning.
- With 792 "replicates" per side, a very small shift can reach q < 0.05. That is why these three ions pass q but show almost no separation.
- The results describe pixels on one section, not biology. Treat them as exploratory only.
- For a real p-value, you must have several sections or animals per group. Then set the unit of replication to "regions / sections" or "animals / patients".
