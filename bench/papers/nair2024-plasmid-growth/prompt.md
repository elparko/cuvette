# Prompts

## Plain
1. I grew a streptomycin-resistant E. coli host with and without 12 clinical plasmids in a plate reader. Do the plasmids slow the growth of this host?
2. Give the relative growth rate of each plasmid against the plasmid-free host, with a confidence interval and a test against 1.

## Guided
Fit a logistic growth curve to each well with growthcurver. Divide the growth rate of each well by the rate of the host well at the top of its plate column. Give the mean relative rate of each plasmid with a 95% interval, a one-sample t test against 1, and the mean of the plasmid means.
