# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have reaction times of 18 people measured on 10 days of sleep restriction. How much slower do people get for each extra day?
2. Do people differ in how fast they slow down? Give me the spread between people.
3. Is the effect of days significant? Show a plot of each person with their line.

## Guided
Does reaction time rise with days of sleep deprivation, and does the rate differ between subjects? Fit a model with a random intercept and a random slope for each subject by REML. The 18 subjects are the independent units. Report the fixed effects, the random-effect standard deviations and the REML criterion.
