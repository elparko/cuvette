# Prompts

## Plain
1. Does age, the pheromone treatment or the replicate change the abdominal protein of these bees? Test all three together.
2. Do the same for the gland acini area.
3. The normalized enzyme activity is not normally distributed. Compare the four treatment groups and the three replicates with a rank test, and give the pairwise comparisons.

## Guided
Nurse-aged bees were held with or without queen mandibular pheromone, at two ages, in three runs of the experiment. Which of the three factors changes the abdominal protein and the gland size?
