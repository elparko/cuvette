# Prompts

## Plain
1. Eighteen people were limited to 3 hours of sleep for 10 days. The file has their reaction time each day. How much slower do they get each day?
2. Give me the change per day with a standard error and a confidence interval.
3. People differ in how fast they start and how fast they slow down. Take that into account.

## Guided
How many milliseconds does reaction time rise for each day of sleep restriction? The 18 subjects each have 10 days of measurements.
