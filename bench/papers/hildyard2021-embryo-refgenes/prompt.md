# Prompts

## Plain
1. I measured 15 candidate reference genes in mouse embryos, heads and forelimbs. Which genes are the most stable?
2. Give the geNorm and NormFinder values, and say how many reference genes I need.
3. What are the efficiencies of the primer pairs?

## Guided
Rank the 15 candidate genes with geNorm and NormFinder over all samples, then with NormFinder grouped by tissue, and with geNorm in the heads only. Give the slope and the efficiency of each primer pair from the dilution series.
