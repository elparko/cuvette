# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have RNA-seq counts from KGN granulosa cells treated with DHT or with vehicle. Which genes change with DHT?
2. How many genes go up and how many go down?
3. Give me a table of the top 20 genes with fold change and significance.

## Guided
Does dihydrotestosterone (DHT) change gene expression in KGN human granulosa cells? I did five independent experiments on different dates. Each experiment gave one DHT culture and one vehicle culture.
