# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have three 10x data sets of cell lines. Put them together and remove the difference between the data sets.
2. How well do the data sets mix before and after the correction? Do the two cell lines stay apart?

## Guided
I have three 10x data sets: pure Jurkat cells, pure HEK293T cells, and a 50:50 mix of both. Clean the cells, remove the difference between the data sets with Harmony, and measure the mixing of the data sets and the separation of the cell lines before and after (iLISI and cLISI).
