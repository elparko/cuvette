# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have a list of molecules with measured solubility. How many of them follow Lipinski's rule of five?
2. Predict solubility from structure with a simple equation and tell me how well it matches the measured values.
3. Which molecules are the biggest outliers?

## Guided
Can a simple equation of size, lipophilicity, flexibility and aromatic content predict the measured aqueous solubility of this set of small molecules?
