# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have a folder of 10x output for blood cells from one healthy donor. Show me what cell types are in it.
2. Clean this single-cell data the usual way and group the cells into clusters. Tell me how many cells you kept and how many clusters you found.
3. Which genes mark each cluster?

## Guided
Which cell populations are in this sample of peripheral blood mononuclear cells, and how many cells pass quality control?
