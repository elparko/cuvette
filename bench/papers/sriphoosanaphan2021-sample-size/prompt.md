# Prompts

## Plain
1. I am planning a vitamin D trial with the change in TGF-beta1 as the outcome. How many patients do I need?
2. The earlier study gives a difference of 81 with an SD of 110. I want 80% power at 5%, two-sided.

## Guided
How many patients per arm does a two-sample t-test need to detect a difference of 81 with an SD of 110, at 80% power and a two-sided alpha of 0.05?
