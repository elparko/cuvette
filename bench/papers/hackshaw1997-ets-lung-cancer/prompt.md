# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have the odds ratios of 37 studies of lung cancer in non-smoking women whose husband smokes. Is the risk higher?
2. Pool the studies and give me the pooled odds ratio with a confidence interval and the heterogeneity.

## Guided
Pool the 37 studies with a random-effects model (DerSimonian-Laird). What is the pooled odds ratio of lung cancer in non-smoking women whose husband smokes, with a 95% confidence interval?
