# Prompts

## Plain
1. 52 healthy adults got endotoxin or placebo. They rated their current health before the injection, at 90 minutes and at 270 minutes. Did endotoxin make them feel less healthy?
2. They also rated their general health once, at 90 minutes. Is it different between the groups?

## Guided
Does an endotoxin injection lower self-rated current health compared with placebo at 90 and 270 minutes, with the subjects as the independent units?
