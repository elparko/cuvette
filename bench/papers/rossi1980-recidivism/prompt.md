# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have a file of 432 men released from prison. It has weeks until re-arrest, whether they were re-arrested, whether they got financial aid, and some background columns. Does financial aid reduce re-arrest?
2. Give me the effect size with a confidence interval and a p-value, after you adjust for age, race, work experience, marriage, parole and prior convictions.
3. How well does the model separate men who are arrested early from men who are not?

## Guided
Does financial aid after release lower the rate of re-arrest in the first 52 weeks, after adjustment for the background columns? Check that the model assumptions hold.
