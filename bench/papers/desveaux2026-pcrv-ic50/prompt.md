# Prompts

## Plain
1. How potent are the anti-PcrV antibodies MEDI3902, 30-B8 and P3D6 in the injection assay and in the cytotoxicity assay?
2. Does 30-B8 block all five PcrV variants with a similar IC50?

## Guided
Fit a three-parameter log-logistic curve (bottom 0) to the normalized response of each antibody and each variant, and give each IC50 with a 95% confidence interval.
