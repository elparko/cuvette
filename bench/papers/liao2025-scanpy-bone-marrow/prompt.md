# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have 10x single-cell data from mouse bone marrow stromal cells, one pulse sample and one chase sample. Clean the data.
2. How many cells does each sample have before and after quality control?

## Guided
I sorted tdTomato-positive stromal cells from the endosteal bone marrow of Adipoq-CreERT2;Ai9 male mice, 24 hours (pulse) or 4 weeks (chase) after tamoxifen. Do the usual cell quality control on each sample separately and tell me how many cells each sample has before and after quality control.
