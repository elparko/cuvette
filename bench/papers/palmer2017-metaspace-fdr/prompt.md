# Prompts

## Plain
1. I imaged three neighbouring mouse brain sections by MALDI. Here are the files. Which molecules can you actually trust in these images?
2. How many different molecules show up in all three sections?
3. Are most of what you find lipids? Which classes?

## Guided
How reproducible is the set of confidently annotated metabolites across serial sections of one mouse brain imaged by high-resolution MALDI?
