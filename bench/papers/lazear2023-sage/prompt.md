# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have three tandem mass spectrometry runs of a standard protein mix, and a protein sequence file for it. Search them and tell me how many peptide matches you can trust at a 1 percent false discovery rate.
2. How many different peptides is that?
3. How many of the matches that you kept are decoys?

## Guided
Which peptides are in this protein mix, how many spectra match them at 1 percent FDR, and how does the number change at 5 percent?
