# Prompts

## Plain
1. I ran an anti-Lassa NP IgG ELISA nine times with a standard curve and a dilution series of a reference serum. How accurate and precise is the assay over its range?
2. Give the mean back-calculated concentration, its CV and the recovery for each dilution and for the two positive controls.
3. What are the LLOQ and the ULOQ?

## Guided
Fit a 4PL standard curve for each run as Gen5 does, back-calculate the dilution series and the controls, and give the inter-assay mean, CV and recovery. Give the LLOQ and the ULOQ from the dilutions with a recovery of 75 to 125 percent and a CV below 25 percent.
