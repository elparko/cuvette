# Prompts

## Plain requests
1. I have H&E images from many organs. Find every nucleus and tell me how many there are per organ.
2. Outline the nuclei in these H&E patches and tell me how close the outlines are to the hand-drawn ones.
3. Which tissue has the most nuclei per image? Use this folder of stained slides.

## Guided variant
Question: How well does an automatic nucleus segmentation of the NuInsSeg H&E patches agree with the manual outlines, measured as Dice and per-organ nucleus counts?
