# Prompts

## Plain requests
1. I have a molecular dynamics run of adenylate kinase. Tell me how big the system is and how long the run is.
2. How far does the protein move away from its starting structure by the end of the run?
3. Does the protein open or close during the run? Show me a plot.
4. I have the open and the closed crystal structures of the same enzyme. How different are they?

## Guided variant
Question: How much does the adenylate kinase backbone move from the first frame to the last frame of the trajectory, and how different are the open (4AKE) and closed (1AKE) crystal structures?
