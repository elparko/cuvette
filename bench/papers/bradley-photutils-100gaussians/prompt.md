# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have a small telescope image, a FITS file with a lot of stars on a flat sky. What is the sky level and how noisy is the sky?
2. How many stars are in it? Find the ones that stand out from the noise.
3. Measure the brightness of the stars at pixel positions (48.70, 200.33), (145.05, 168.52) and (355.56, 252.20). Take out the sky.

## Guided
What are the sky level and sky noise of this image, how many sources stand out from the noise, and what are the net aperture fluxes of the three stars at the listed pixel positions? State every setting that you used.
