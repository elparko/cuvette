# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have a concatenated ITS, LSU, tub2 and tef1-a alignment of Sporocadaceae fungi. Build the maximum-likelihood tree.
2. Which reference species do my Cyprus isolates group with?
3. Give me the model and the log-likelihood of the tree.

## Guided
I isolated Sporocadaceae fungi from grapevine trunk cankers in Cyprus and sequenced four loci. Build the maximum-likelihood tree with branch support from the concatenated alignment, rooted on Beltrania rhombica CBS 123.58. Tell me the number of sequences and sites, the model and the log-likelihood.
