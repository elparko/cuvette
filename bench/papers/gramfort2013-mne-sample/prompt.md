# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have a brain recording from one person who heard tones in the left ear and saw flashes. The file is the MNE sample data. How many events of each kind are in it?
2. Cut the recording around each event and drop the bad stretches the usual way. How many auditory left-ear trials are left?
3. When does the brain response to the left-ear tone peak, about a tenth of a second after the tone? How big is it?

## Guided
How many events of each type does the sample recording hold, how many epochs stay after rejection, and when and how strong is the N100 to the left-ear tone in the gradiometers?
