# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have two lists of intervals on human chromosome 1 (genome build hg18). The first is RefSeq exons. The second is AluY repeats. How many exons overlap a repeat, and how many bases overlap in total?
2. Now count only the overlaps where at least half of the exon is covered by the repeat. How many pairs are left?
3. Merge the repeats that lie within 100 bases of each other. How many intervals remain?

## Guided
Do exons on chromosome 1 overlap AluY repeats more than a quick look suggests, and how much does the answer depend on how much overlap I require?
