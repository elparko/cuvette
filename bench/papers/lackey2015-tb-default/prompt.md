# Prompts

## Plain
1. I followed adults with tuberculosis in Lima and recorded who defaulted from treatment. Which patient characteristics go with default?
2. Test each characteristic, then give me the adjusted odds ratios of the final model.

## Guided
Which factors are associated with tuberculosis treatment default? Give the chi-square p-value of age group, sex, marital status and poverty, and the adjusted odds ratios with 95% confidence intervals of a logistic regression on drug use, BMI, MDR treatment, weekly alcohol, HIV status and secondary education.
