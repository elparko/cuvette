# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have a table of plant species cover at 20 meadow sites, and a table that says how each site is managed. How diverse is the first site?
2. Do the four management types have different plant communities? Give me a p-value and the share of variation that management explains.
3. Draw a map of the sites that shows how similar they are, and tell me how reliable the map is.

## Guided
Does the plant community of Dutch dune meadows differ between the four management types, and is that difference a difference in community composition rather than in spread? Use the Bray-Curtis distance, 999 permutations and a fixed seed. The 20 sites are the independent units.
