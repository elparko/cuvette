# Prompts

## Plain
1. I tested E. coli and K. pneumoniae isolates against piperacillin/tazobactam and temocillin. How many isolates are susceptible in each group, and what are the MIC50 and the MIC90?
2. Split the isolates by resistance to third-generation cephalosporins.

## Guided
Interpret the MICs of piperacillin/tazobactam and temocillin with the EUCAST 2021 breakpoints. Count S and I together as susceptible. Give the number of isolates, the MIC50, the MIC90 and the percent susceptible for each species and each cephalosporin group.
