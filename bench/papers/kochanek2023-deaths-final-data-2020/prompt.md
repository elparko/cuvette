# Prompts

## Plain
1. Did the death rate in the United States go up in 2020? Adjust for age.
2. Are men and women dying at the same rate once you account for age?
3. Is the 2020 rise a real change or an effect of an older population?

## Guided
Did the age-adjusted death rate of the United States rise from 2019 to 2020, and how do men and women compare? The file nchs_2020_age_rates.csv has the death rate per 100,000 in 11 age groups for five groups: all causes 2020, all causes 2019, heart disease 2020, males 2020 and females 2020. The column std_pop_2000 holds the year 2000 United States standard population. The file has rates and no counts. Give the age-adjusted death rate of each group with the direct method, the ratio of 2020 to 2019 and the ratio of the male rate to the female rate.
