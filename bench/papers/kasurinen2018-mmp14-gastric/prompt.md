# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have 240 gastric cancer patients with a serum MMP-14 level before surgery, split into a low and a high group. Do patients with a high MMP-14 die of gastric cancer sooner?
2. Give me the 5-year disease-specific survival in each group and the log-rank p.
3. Give me the hazard ratio for a high MMP-14 with a confidence interval and a p-value, without adjustment and after adjustment for age, stage and Lauren type.
4. Does the effect differ between the intestinal and the diffuse type?

## Guided
Is a high serum MMP-14 before surgery an independent predictor of disease-specific death in gastric cancer, after adjustment for age, stage and Lauren type?
