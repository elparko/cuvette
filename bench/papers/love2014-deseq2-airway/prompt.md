# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have RNA-seq counts from airway muscle cells, with and without the steroid dexamethasone. Which genes change with the drug?
2. Find the genes that go up or down with treatment. How many are there?
3. Give me a table of the top 20 genes with fold change and significance.

## Guided
Does dexamethasone change gene expression in human airway smooth muscle cells, and in which genes? The samples come from four donors, each with a treated and an untreated culture.
