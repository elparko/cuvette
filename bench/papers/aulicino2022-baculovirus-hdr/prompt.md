# Prompts

## Plain
1. I edited HEK293T cells with a baculovirus and read mCherry by flow cytometry. What percent of the cells are edited with each design?
2. Is the HITI-2c design better than the HDR design?

## Guided
Set the mCherry cutoff at the 99.9th percentile of the pooled untransfected control events in the channel PE-CF594-A. Count the cells above it in each sample. Give the mean and SD of the percent positive of each design and the p value of a Welch t test.
