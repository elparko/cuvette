# Prompts

## Plain requests
1. This is a stained slide of lymph node tissue. What percent of the cells are positive for the brown stain?
2. Count the cells in this prostate slide and tell me how they are spread across the tissue.
3. Find the tissue on these three prostate slides, skip the empty glass, and give me the tissue area in square millimeters.

## Guided variant
Question: In the Ki-67 stained lymphoma slide, what fraction of tumor cells have a positive nucleus, and how much does that fraction change if the staining cutoff moves?
