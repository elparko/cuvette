# Prompts

## Plain
1. I ran a randomized trial of vitamin D against placebo for 6 weeks in 75 patients after hepatitis C cure. I measured 25(OH)D and four fibrosis markers at baseline and at week 6. Did vitamin D work?
2. For each marker, give me the difference between the arms with a confidence interval and a p-value.

## Guided
Compared with placebo, does 6 weeks of vitamin D change the serum fibrosis markers TGF-beta1, TIMP-1, MMP-9 and P3NP from baseline?
