# Prompts

## Plain
1. There are two public mouse brain lipid MALDI datasets on METASPACE. How many lipids can you confidently identify in each?
2. Do the two datasets agree on which lipids are present?
3. Which lipid ions look most localized in a part of the brain?

## Guided
How many lipid annotations does each public mouse brain dataset support at a controlled false discovery rate, and how does the count change with the database?
