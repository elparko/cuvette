# Prompts

## Plain
1. I have a Visium sample of human cortex. Which genes follow the tissue structure?
2. How many spots are in the tissue, and where do MOBP and SNAP25 rank?

## Guided
I have one 10x Visium sample of the human dorsolateral prefrontal cortex. Remove the genes that few spots express, normalize, link the spots on the Visium grid and rank the genes by Moran's I. Tell me the number of spots in the tissue, the number of genes that pass the filter, and the rank of the layer genes MOBP and SNAP25.
