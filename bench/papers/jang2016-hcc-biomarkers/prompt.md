# Prompts

## Plain
1. I measured AFP, PIVKA-II, osteopontin and Dickkopf-1 in patients with HCC and with cirrhosis. Which marker finds HCC best?
2. Give me the AUC of each marker with a confidence interval, and the sensitivity and specificity at the usual cut-offs.

## Guided
For each of the four serum markers, what is the AUC for HCC against cirrhosis with a DeLong 95% confidence interval? What are the sensitivity and specificity at AFP 20 ng/mL, PIVKA-II 10 ng/mL, OPN 100 ng/mL and DKK-1 500 pg/mL?
