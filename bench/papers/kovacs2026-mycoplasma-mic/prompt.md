# Prompts

## Plain
1. I measured nine drugs against 110 strains of a Mycoplasma species. What are the MIC50 and the MIC90 of each drug?
2. Some values are limits of the dilution series, such as <=0.25 or >64.

## Guided
Give the MIC50 and the MIC90 of each drug. The MIC50 is the lowest concentration that inhibits at least half of the strains. The MIC90 is the lowest concentration that inhibits at least 90% of the strains. Rank a value with a > sign above the same number without a sign.
