# Non-inferiority

1. A non-inferiority trial asks if the new treatment loses no more than a margin against the control. The margin comes from the protocol.
2. Ask for the margin, its scale and the one-sided level. The scale is a risk difference, a risk ratio, a hazard ratio or a loss of restricted mean survival time.
3. Ask if the event harms or helps. For a harmful event, the upper limit of the interval must lie below the margin. For a helpful event, the lower limit must lie above minus the margin.
4. Run the tool with the margin. Read the limit in the summary and compare it with the margin.
5. If the interval crosses the margin, the data do not show non-inferiority. Do not widen the margin.
6. Report the analysis population. Run non-inferiority on the per protocol population and on the intention to treat population.
7. Write the margin, its source, the limit, the level and the conclusion in the answer.
